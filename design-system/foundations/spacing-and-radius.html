Spacing & Radius

A 4px-based spacing scale and a deliberately square default. Corners stay sharp unless a component opts into a radius — which keeps the dense, IDE-like surfaces feeling precise rather than soft.

Borders

Hairlines are 0.5px on retina. The focus ring is a 1.5px highlight-border outline, inset.

Signature corner-bracket border. Outlined controls draw an L-bracket at each corner in the full currentColor and the rest of the frame at 50% opacity — a small, recognizable detail that shows up on secondary/outline buttons and toolbar chips. See it live on the Buttons card.
