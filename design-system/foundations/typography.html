Typography

Three families: Funnel Display for headings (geometric, a touch of character), Inter for body, and Geist Mono for code, table headers, keys, and IDs. Body weights are deliberately fine-grained — Medium 475 / Regular 425 / Book 375 — to tune density without changing size.
