Review-state cells

Every field moves through three states — published (live in the service), approved (staged), and local (the working edit). The grid encodes this on the cell itself: a 4px left rail in the state's stroke over a colored wash. Two axes read at a glance — the kind of change (modified / created / deleted) by hue, and the stage (needs-review vs approved) by saturation.
