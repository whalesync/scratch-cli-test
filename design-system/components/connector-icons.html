Connector icons

A connector's identity is its service logo, loaded from connector metadata and rendered in a fixed square that contains (never crops) the artwork. The component takes a size and optional padding; GENERIC_API has no logo, so it falls back to a pocket-knife glyph, and a failed or missing logo renders nothing (or a neutral monogram in lists). Monograms below stand in for real logos.

Sizes
